Crafted without plastic microfibers or petrochemical-based synthetics, this product is exclusively made from 100% pure merino fibres, meticulously chosen for their strength and softness. This results in a luxurious fabric that not only naturally breathes and resists odours but also aligns with sustainable principles. Additionally, merino wool fibre is inherently renewable, recyclable, and, under specific conditions, biodegradable.

Key benefits

  • Naturally odour-resistant
  • No-itch comfort
  • Regulates body temperature

Sustainability Commitment

Icebreaker, founded in New Zealand in 1995, is a pioneer in sustainable and natural performance clothing. The merino wool is 100% traceable and is obtained without the mulesing process usually used for merino and therefore without cruelty to animals. We have compiled further information on the information page on Icebreaker.

Features

  • Soft merino fibres that regulate body temperature and naturally resist odour
  • Offset shoulder seams to prevent friction
