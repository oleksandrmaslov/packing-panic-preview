  • Description

The SQZY pack sack from Tatonka is ideal for keeping your travel utensils sorted and can also be used as a storage bag in everyday life. The square bottom makes it easy to pack. The water-repellent material protects the contents from splashing water.

Features

  • Type: stuff sack or stuff sack, not waterproof
  • Drawstring with cord stopper
  • Hanging tab
  • Washable
  • Extremely light and tear-resistant material
  • PFC/PFAS-free impregnation
  • Splashproof
  • Material: siliconized polyamide fabric
  • Capacity: 5l
  • Weight, according to manufacturer: 25g
  • Dimensions, according to manufacturer: 30 x 17.5 x 10 cm
