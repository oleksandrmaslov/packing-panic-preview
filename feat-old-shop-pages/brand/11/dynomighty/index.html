Dynomighty is a brand specialising in wallets, card holders, and bags crafted from DuPont™ Tyvek®, a material known for its tear resistance, water resistance, and recyclability. These products are designed to be thinner, lighter, and stronger than traditional leather wallets, featuring a stitchless origami construction that allows them to expand as needed.

Dynomighty is dedicated to environmental responsibility through:

  • Recyclable Materials: Tyvek® products can be recycled and repurposed into new items, such as park benches and playground equipment.
  • Recycling Program: Dynomighty has recycled over 5,000 wallets to date and offers a program for customers to send back used Tyvek® wallets for recycling.
