Since 1974, Osprey has been designing innovative and durable backpacks and travel gear. Founded by Mike Pfotenhauer in Santa Cruz, California, the company began with handmade, custom-fit packs and has grown into a globally recognised brand.

Osprey utilises lightweight, durable materials and ergonomic designs to ensure comfort and functionality. Features like adjustable harnesses and sustainable fabrics enhance their products' performance.

At Packing Panic, we’ve curated a selection of Osprey’s most lightweight and practical products, including travel backpacks, daypacks, dry bags, and packing cubes. Each item is chosen to optimise organisation, durability, and efficiency for travellers.

Osprey is dedicated to reducing its environmental impact through:

  • Recycled Materials: 99% of products use 100% recycled main fabrics; 100% use recycled liners (Fall 2024).
  • PFAS-Free: All textile products now use PFAS-free DWR.
  • bluesign®-Approved: Most fabrics meet bluesign® standards for safer chemicals.
  • Ethical Practices: Fair labour and responsible production standards.

Find out their sustainability pledge, here.

Osprey's dedication to creating durable, lightweight, and sustainable gear aligns with Packing Panic's mission to optimise travel essentials for efficient and enjoyable journeys.
