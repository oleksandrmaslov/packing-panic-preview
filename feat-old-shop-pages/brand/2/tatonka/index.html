Founded in Germany in 1980, Tatonka is a leading outdoor equipment brand celebrated for its commitment to quality and transparency. Specialising in backpacks, tents, cooking gear, and clothing, Tatonka maintains exceptional standards through its SA8000-certified factories in Vietnam. Its Open Factory programme, which allows public visits, reflects the brand’s dedication to ethical practices and fair production. Combining durability with social responsibility, Tatonka is a trusted name in outdoor gear.

At Packing Panic, we’ve curated Tatonka’s lightweight, travel-friendly products, including discreet travel belts, ultra-light dry bags, and compact packing sacks. These versatile essentials are designed for travellers prioritising organisation, practicality, and minimal luggage weight.

Tatonka’s sustainability efforts include:

  • GREEN Label Products: Made with recycled and renewable materials, including organic cotton and kapok.
  • PFC-Free Clothing: Free of harmful chemicals since 2016.
  • Renewable Energy: Photovoltaic systems power 65% of its Vietnam factory’s energy needs.
  • Open Factory: Highest social standards and transparency

Tatonka’s dedication to ethical manufacturing and sustainability aligns seamlessly with Packing Panic’s mission to provide responsible, high-quality travel solutions.
